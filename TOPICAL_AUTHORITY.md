# Topical Authority — jati.furnitur.co.id

## Role and boundary

`jati.furnitur.co.id` is a specialist Indonesian knowledge and commercial-support property for teak furniture, doors, gazebos, and traditional Javanese timber structures. It should help homeowners, designers, buyers, craftspeople, and property operators understand material quality, specification, construction, finishing, care, and procurement. Existing product, portfolio, contact, and product-family routes retain transactional intent. Timber species and grade, moisture and treatment, joinery and structural load, adhesives and finishes, indoor-air claims, legality/certification and sustainability, durability, standards, and prices must be supported by current testable evidence, applicable official/primary sources, and competent technical review before publication; no article substitutes for structural engineering, manufacturer instructions, or project-specific inspection.

## Internal-link rule

Every article links upward to its JAT topic hub. JAT-01–JAT-07 form the shared specification layer and link into the relevant product hub in JAT-08–JAT-13. Product pages link forward to durability and maintenance (JAT-03/JAT-14), legality (JAT-15), and procurement (JAT-16) only where relevant. Diagnostic pages link to the owning material, joint, finish, or product guide before repair advice. Commercial calls to action point to an existing product-family, `/portfolio/`, or `/kontak-kami/` route; no knowledge article duplicates a city template.
